        How to play & keyboard shortcuts

        Blackjack: get closer to 21 than the dealer without going over. Face cards count as 10, Aces count as 1 or 11, whichever helps you most.

        You start on Easy mode. Each time your balance reaches that level's target, you automatically move up — Normal, Medium, Hard, and beyond — where the dealer hits on soft 17 and blackjack payouts tighten toward 6:5. Your current level is shown at the top of the screen.
